My Story

The journey that
shaped who I am

01

I didn't start out wanting to be a designer. I wanted to understand why some products feel effortless and others feel like a fight. That curiosity became a career.

Every chapter below is a turning point — a moment that pushed my thinking forward and shaped how I approach every project today.

2017 – 2020

The Foundation

Growing up in Lagos, I was drawn to the space between people and the systems built to serve them. I studied Civil Engineering at Obafemi Awolowo University, but it was in my final year that I discovered design and technology — and something immediately clicked. where I discovered that what I really loved wasn't building systems, but shaping how people experience them

But somewhere between data structures and algorithms, I realised I cared less about the machines and more about the people using them. After graduating, I threw myself fully into learning the craft. What started as curiosity became a career, shaped by the same principles engineering taught me: understand the problem deeply, design for the people who live with the solution, and never lose sight of who you're building for.

2020 – 2023

Learning to Build & Design

While finishing my degree, I joined Future Academy Africa's competitive bootcamp — selected from thousands of applicants — and simultaneously completed Google's UX Design Professional Certificate, here I built a solid foundation in UI and product design. A few year down the line, i took a frontend development course at UDEMY from Angela Liu who was my instructor. For the first time, I could speak both languages: design and code. I stopped thinking of them as separate disciplines and started seeing them as one.

That hybrid perspective became my biggest asset. I could sketch a wireframe in the morning and ship it as a working component by evening.

2025 – 2026

Going Global with Tech

Being selected for school scholarship with Ts Academy to learn Frontend development was the icing on the cake for me.. It forced me to write cleaner code, communicate across time zones, and understand how large-scale product decisions get made. I grew more in 3 months there than in 3 years before it.

I also began noticing how rarely engineering teams had anyone who could bridge design intent and implementation - and decided that's exactly where I wanted to live.

2026 – Now

Building for Africa & Beyond

Since going independent in 2022, I've worked with startups across Lagos, Abuja, Ogun State, and have designed at national level in the education sector in Nigeri - helping them design and ship products that actually work for their users. Whether it's a fintech app for first-generation bankers in Lagos or an LMS for rural schools without reliable internet, I care deeply about building for the context, not just the prototype.

I'm not done. I'm just getting started.

What I Stand For

The principles that
guide my work

01
🎯
Clarity over cleverness

A great interface is invisible. I resist the urge to show off in favour of making things that just work — for everyone, every time.

02
🧪
Test early, ship often

I'd rather put a rough prototype in front of a real user on day three than present a polished design on day thirty that solves the wrong problem.

03
🌍
Context is everything

A product built for Silicon Valley doesn't automatically work in Lagos. I design for the actual environment — bandwidth, literacy, device, culture.

04
🤝
Collaboration > heroics

The best outcomes come from genuine partnership with clients, engineers, and end users — not from a designer working in isolation and presenting a "reveal."

05
♿
Accessibility is non-negotiable

Designing only for people who look like you is a failure of imagination. I build inclusive products from the first wireframe, not as a final audit.

06
📈
Outcomes over outputs

I care about whether the thing I shipped actually moved the needle — not just that it shipped on time and looked good in the case study.

How I Work

My design & build process

1
Discover

User interviews, analytics audits, competitive analysis, and stakeholder workshops to understand the real problem — not the assumed one.

2
Define

Synthesise findings into a clear brief, user journey maps, and measurable success metrics before any design work begins.

3
Design & Build

Rapid wireframes → interactive prototypes → high-fidelity screens → production code. Each stage is validated with real users.

4
Iterate

Post-launch monitoring, A/B tests, and follow-up research sessions. Shipping is the beginning of the process, not the end.
