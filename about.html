My story

From structures to screens.

01

Every product I design starts the same way it did in engineering school: understand the constraints, then build something that holds up under real use.

2012 — 2019

A foundation in Civil Engineering

I trained as a civil engineer — an OND at Moshood Abiola Polytechnic and a B.Sc at Obafemi Awolowo University (Second Class Upper, 4.09 CGPA). It gave me a way of thinking that never left: break a complex system into parts, respect the constraints, and design for the person who has to live with the result.

2021 — 2022

The pivot into design

I moved into product design deliberately — through Google's UX Design programme, AQSkills, and Future Academy Africa, layering on project management and UX foundations. The engineering mindset turned out to be an advantage: I could hold both the systems view and the human view at once.

2022 — now

Designing and shipping products

Across Creatifff, Ajiroba Technologies, and now Morgen Green, I've designed e-commerce, fashion, SaaS and EduTech products end-to-end — and increasingly carried them into working frontend and analytics, so the gap between design file and shipped product keeps shrinking.
